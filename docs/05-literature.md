# 05. Literature

## Universal Declaration of Human Rights, Article 1 / 世界人權宣言第一條

> All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights. They are endowed with reason and conscience and should act towards one another in a spirit of brotherhood.

**ken ki uril mat in isat etrit in lih po.**
[kɛn ki ʔu.ʁil mɑt ʔin ʔi.ɕɑt ʔɛt.ʁit ʔin lix pɔ]
PERSON ALL BE_BORN FREE AND EQUAL DIGNITY AND RIGHT_(ENTITLEMENT) IN .

**lo vö lol in usan, in vi vih etsël seh sot kut po.**
[lɔ ʋø lɔl ʔin ʔu.ɕɑn ʔin ʋi ʋix ʔɛt.ɕɤl ɕɛx ɕɔt kut pɔ]
THEY HAVE REASON AND CONSCIENCE , AND DO SHOULD EACH_OTHER TOWARDS SPIRIT_(ATTITUDE) BROTHER IN .

Notes:

- "are endowed with" is said as "have": there is no obligatory passive.
- "brotherhood" is SPIRIT BROTHER (possessor after the noun).
- The subject of the second clause is dropped after AND.
