# 02. Orthography

## Alphabet / 字母

One letter per sound.

| Letter | m | n | p | t | k | s | h | v | r | l |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IPA | /m/ | /n/ | /p/ | /t/ | /k/ | /ɕ/ | /x/ | /ʋ/ | /ʁ/ | /l/ |

| Letter | i | ö | e | u | ë | o | a |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IPA | /i/ | /ø/ | /ɛ/ | /u/ | /ɤ/ | /ɔ/ | /ɑ/ |

- Only n, t, h, l can end a syllable.
- An empty consonant is not written: (Ø)ö(Ø) is written *ö*. The glottal stop at the start of a word is not written either.

## Writing words / 書寫

- Words are separated by spaces.
- A word splits into syllables in one way, because every syllable after the first starts with a consonant: {{w: DIGNITY}} "dignity" = {{h: DIGNITY}}. Hyphens are used only in tables to show syllables.
- Compound concepts are written as separate words: {{w: COCONUT_TREE}} "coconut tree" (TREE COCONUT).
- Punctuation follows the word: `.` `,` `?` `!`

## Notes / 注意

- **ö** and **ë** are separate letters, not accented o and e.
- Type them as single characters (Unicode NFC: ö U+00F6, ë U+00EB).
